## Brand & Style

This design system captures the sensory joy of an artisanal gelato cremeria: luxurious, tactile, inviting, and playfully sophisticated. It merges the warmth of an old-world European gelateria with the clean lines of contemporary boutique commerce. 

The aesthetic is characterized as **Tactile Modern-Boutique**:
- **Plush Tactility:** Buttons, chips, and cards possess an inviting, soft-scoop curvature with dimensional, pillowy press states that evoke tactile indulgence.
- **Warm Culinary Atmosphere:** Avoiding sterile cold whites and stark blacks, the experience is bathed in steeped warm milk tones, rich espresso typography, and delectable, flavor-forward pastel accents.
- **Editorial Elegance:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke editorial gastronomy, paired with an ultra-clean geometric sans-serif to maintain effortless e-commerce utility and checkout clarity.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from pure culinary ingredients: steeped cream, stone-ground cocoa, wild berries, and Bronte pistachios. 

- **Primary (`#4A2E2B` - Artisan Dark Chocolate):** Used for primary buttons, grounding structural elements, and high-emphasis brand moments. Represents craft, richness, and depth.
- **Secondary (`#E76F7E` - Wild Strawberry):** An energetic, appetizing berry hue used for featured badges, active highlights, celebratory banners, and interactive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#72B085` - Pistachio Cream):** A soft, herbal green for dietary tags (vegan, dairy-free, organic), success notifications, and secondary balance.
- **Neutral (`#241B18` - Roasted Espresso):** Deep, warm near-black for high-contrast, readable typography and crisp iconography.
- **Canvas & Surface System:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FDFBF7` (Fior di Latte / Warm Milk)
  - Surface Raised / Cards: `#FAF6F0` (Clotted Cream)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F2EAE0` (Toasted Almond)
- **Accent Tints:**
  - Mango Vanilla: `#FDC453` (Limited releases, ratings, seasonal alerts)
  - Raspberry Mousse: `#F49DAB` (Soft hover states, tint backgrounds)
  - Mint Wash: `#E8F5EC` (Dietary badge backgrounds)

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances artisanal heritage with brisk modern legibility:
- **Playfair Display (Serif):** Handles editorial expression, product flavor titles, flavor story intros, and campaign taglines. Its high stroke contrast evokes bespoke confectionery packaging.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Sans-Serif):** Modern, open, and friendly geometry. Used for catalog scanning, nutritional tables, prices, buttons, and checkout interactions to guarantee effortless utility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a fluid 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) centered within a maximum width of 1440px.

- **Airy Rhythm:** Spacing is generous to preserve a relaxed, high-end patisserie browsing experience. Sections are decoupled with `space-xl` to double-spacing rhythm.
- **Reflow Behavior:** Product flavor grids gracefully shift from a 4-column display on wide desktop screens to a 2-column card layout on tablet, and a horizontal snap-carousel or full-bleed single column cards on mobile devices.
- **Inner Padding:** Cards and dialog containers employ generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to mirror the soft, pillowy spatial philosophy.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with an ambient, sunlit quality—reminiscent of soft morning light streaming through a gelateria window:

- **Warm-Tinted Ambient Shadows:** Shadows reject neutral grays in favor of dark espresso undertones (`rgba(36, 27, 24, 0.06)` to `rgba(36, 27, 24, 0.12)`), preventing dirty or murky interfaces.
- **Tactile Scooped Elevation (Plush Tiering):**
  - **Level 1 (Resting Cards):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(74, 46, 43, 0.05)`, edged with a subtle border of `rgba(74, 46, 43, 0.04)`.
  - **Level 2 (Hover / Floating UI / Modals):** `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(74, 46, 43, 0.10)`, accompanied by an upward -2px translateY shift to mimic buoyant creaminess.
  - **Level 3 (Tactile Press / Active):** Drop shadows compress to `0 2px 6px rgba(74, 46, 43, 0.08)` with a 1px downward translation, conveying responsive physical dampening.
- **Glass Accents:** Translucent elements (sticky navigation bars, flavor tag overlays) employ `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a warm tint (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped, fully rounded philosophy (`roundedness: 3`):
- **Buttons, Badges, and Input Fields:** Full pill contours (`border-radius: 9999px`) to mirror scoops and polished wooden spoons.
- **Product & Story Cards:** Curvature starts at `2rem` (32px), eliminating sharp corners in favor of gentle, organic contours.
- **Images and Media:** Product hero cutouts and flavor photographs are housed within soft pebble frames, pill masks, or 32px rounded squircles.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Filled Dark Chocolate (`#4A2E2B`) with Fior di Latte text (`#FDFBF7`). Fully rounded pill shape, horizontal padding `space-lg`, vertical padding `space-sm`. On hover, lifts smoothly; on press, translates down 1px with reduced shadow.
  - *Secondary / Flavor Action:* Wild Strawberry (`#E76F7E`) or Mango Vanilla (`#FDC453`) background with dark espresso text.
  - *Ghost / Outline:* Transparent base, 1.5px border of `#4A2E2B`, with soft almond hover wash (`#F2EAE0`).
- **Flavor Chips & Dietary Badges:**
  - Pill containers using pastel tint backgrounds: Mint Wash (`#E8F5EC`) with dark Pistachio text (`#2A5936`), or Strawberry Silk (`#FCEEF0`) with Wild Strawberry text.
  - Compact typography (`label-md`), high horizontal padding (`space-md`), zero harsh outlines.
- **Input Fields & Quantity Steppers:**
  - Form fields use Surface Raised background (`#FAF6F0`), subtle 1px border (`#E5DCD0`), and 9999px pill radius.
  - Focus state applies a 2px outer glow in Wild Strawberry (`rgba(231, 111, 126, 0.25)`) with a chocolate border.
  - Pint/Scoop Quantity Stepper: Pill container encapsulating minus, counter, and plus, with micro-click spring feedback.
- **Product Flavor Cards:**
  - Contained in Clotted Cream (`#FAF6F0`) surfaces with `rounded-xl` corners.
  - Top visual area showcases floating, drop-shadowed pint photography or scoop cross-sections.
  - Content block contains flavor notes in serif (`headline-md`), tasting profile chips, and a quick-add pill button.
- **Lists & Nutrition Panels:**
  - Nutrition tables and ingredient lists utilize thin, soft divider borders (`rgba(74, 46, 43, 0.08)`) with ample vertical padding.
- **Tasting Flight Builder (Custom Component):**
  - A 4-slot modular dock rendered at the screen bottom with translucent frosted milk glass (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)`), allowing users to drop and customize artisanal pint bundles with haptic-inspired animation states.
